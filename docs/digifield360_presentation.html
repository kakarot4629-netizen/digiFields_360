Feature Deep Dive

1. Autonomous AI Dispatching

Eliminating dispatch delays through multi-variable heuristic scoring.

Multi-Variable Scoring Algorithm

Every incoming ticket evaluates available technicians using weighted mathematical scoring:

  • 35% Skills & Certification: Matches required OEM certs in Technician_Skill__c.
  • 30% Proximity: Live GPS calculation to customer site.
  • 20% Workload Balancing: Prevents shift burnout by analyzing active job queues.
  • 15% Customer Tier & CSAT: Prioritizes top-rated technicians for VIP clients.

Supervisor Drag-and-Drop Console

Built as a modern Lightning Web Component (df360DispatchBoard):

  • Live Kanban columns by technician status (Available, On Job, Travelling).
  • Instant visual drag-and-drop reassignment with supervisor override.
  • Automatic publication to DF360_Job_Assigned__e platform event bus.
  • Zero-latency dispatch notification to technician mobile within 60 seconds.
Feature Deep Dive

2. Offline-First Mobile Application

Guaranteed field execution in basements, industrial plants, and zero-connectivity zones.

🌅

Morning Pre-Load

At shift start, the app downloads today's work orders, asset histories, troubleshooting guides, and parts catalogs into local storage.

Feature Deep Dive

3. Real-Time SLA Engine & Escalation

Proactively protecting contractual margins and customer trust.

⚠️

80% Yellow Alert

When 80% of allowed resolution time passes on an open job, an alert fires on DF360_SLA_Alert__e. Supervisor is alerted, and GuruAI recommends immediate intervention.

🚨

100% Breach Red Alert

If SLA breaches, the job status switches to Breached. A proactive apology WhatsApp message is dispatched to the customer with an updated ETA.

🔄

Repeat-Visit RCA

If an asset experiences $\ge 3$ service jobs in 30 days, it is automatically escalated to a Senior Engineer for Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

Feature Deep Dive

4. Multi-Depot Spare Parts Management

Eliminating parts chase and boosting First-Time Fix Rates (FTFR).

Field Requisitions & Mobile Catalog

Technicians search the live catalog on mobile, checking stock across regional warehouses (Depot__c) and raising requisitions on-site.

  • Auto-Approval (≤ ₹5,000): Instant automated stock reservation at the nearest depot.
  • Supervisor Approval (> ₹5,000): Routed to the df360PartsApproval desk queue.

Ledger & Replenishment Automation

Integrated inventory ledger tracking:

  • Tracks Quantity_Available__c and Quantity_Reserved__c.
  • Separates warranty coverage (₹0 charge) from billable consumer parts.
  • Automated procurement alerts when depot inventory drops below Reorder_Level__c.
